How It Works

Simple Setup, Accurate Tracking

Getting started takes less than a minute.

1

Insert the Smart Insole

Place the S.T.E.P.S insole into any shoe. It's thin, comfortable, and designed to fit standard shoe sizes without changing how your shoes feel.

2

Connect via Bluetooth

Open the S.T.E.P.S app and tap connect. The insole pairs instantly and begins tracking automatically. No complicated setup required.

3

Walk and Track

Every step you take is counted in real-time and synced to your phone. Watch your progress, hit your goals, and trust that your numbers are accurate.
